{\ttfamily listdef=true} indicates that the atoms are primarily determined by an overloading of {\ttfamily atoms\+\_\+are} while {\ttfamily atom\+\_\+is} and {\ttfamily natom\+\_\+is} need not be overoaded as they use {\ttfamily atoms\+\_\+are}.
