\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\subsubsection*{template$<$typename E, typename D, typename Z$>$\newline
class orpp\+::ivdistribution$<$ E, D, Z $>$}
Iteratively defined std\+::vector distribution. 


\begin{DoxyTemplParams}{Template Parameters}
{\em D} & conditional distribution of the std\+::vector\textquotesingle{}s component \\
\hline
{\em Z} & mapping from a history of the distribution into the condition of {\ttfamily D} \\
\hline
\end{DoxyTemplParams}
The distribution is defned iteratively, the {\itshape i-\/th} component being conditioned by {\ttfamily \{Z\}(s)}\$ where {\ttfamily s} is the scenario containing previous values of the std\+::vector. Template parameter {\ttfamily Z} has to be a descendant of \doxylink{classorpp_1_1zeta}{zeta}\texorpdfstring{$<$}{<}{\ttfamily X}, {\ttfamily C\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>}} where {\ttfamily X} is the type of {\ttfamily D\textquotesingle{}s} values and {\ttfamily C} is the type of its condition.
